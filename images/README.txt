Drop your images into this folder with these exact filenames:

  background.jpg   <- your hero background image
  photo1.jpg
  photo2.jpg
  photo3.jpg
  photo4.jpg
  photo5.jpg
  photo6.jpg

Tips:
- JPG, PNG, and WEBP all work. If you use a different extension,
  update the filenames in index.html and style.css to match.
- Square-ish photos look best for the polaroids (they'll be
  cropped to a square via CSS).
- Background works best as a wide landscape photo (at least 1920px wide).
- Any missing photo will show a "add photo" placeholder until you drop it in.
